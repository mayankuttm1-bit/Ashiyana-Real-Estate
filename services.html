Service Spotlight 03

Property Doctor Advisory — Consult Before You Buy

Buying property without proper forensic title checks is the #1 reason investors in India lose money to fraud, illegal layouts, and generational litigation.

Our Property Doctor division acts as your independent legal shield. We conduct exhaustive 30-year registrar searches, check Nazul lease conditions, verify master-plan land use diversion (Vyavsayik / Aawasiya), and physically verify Khasra coordinates before you sign any agreement.

30-Year Chain Audit Trace all prior owners and registered deeds across the Jabalpur District Registrar records.
Diversion & Master Plan Confirm the land is officially diverted under MP Land Revenue Code with valid Town & Country permissions.
Fraud Shield Check Identify disputed inheritance, fake POA, mortgage liens, or double sale attempts.
Site Demarcation Audit Ensure the physical boundary matches the exact Khasra map at the Tehsil office.

Service Spotlight 04

Land Mutation (Namantaran) & Government Registrations

Signing a sale deed is only halfway through ownership. Complete legal possession only occurs once your name is officially mutated in the Revenue Khasra/Khatauni records at the Tehsil and Municipal Corporation.

Ashiyana provides end-to-end liaison with the Jabalpur District Registrar Office, Tehsil Courts, and Jabalpur Nagar Nigam. We draft legally impregnable deeds, calculate exact stamp duties, and follow through until the mutated Rin Pustika (B-1/P-II) is handed to you.

Tehsil Mutation (Namantaran): Name transfer in revenue records without administrative delays.
Nagar Nigam Property Tax Registration: Seamless municipal assessment.
Building Permissions: Documentation support for municipal maps and NOCs.
